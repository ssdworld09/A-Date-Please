For You About Me One Question

for Revati

I've been
paying attention.

The first time we properly talked, it didn't feel like a first time at all. Since then I have been quietly collecting little things about you; here are some of them.

  • You call me cute

    And I like it more than I will ever admit out loud. I don't know why it lands the way it does; but it does, every single time. So please don't stop.

  • You love flowers

    So now I can't walk past them. I catch myself photographing the ones in the supermarket aisle; phone out before I have even decided to; just because I know they will make you smile.

  • You can sleep anywhere

    Mid-sentence, mid-plan, mid-anything. And I have quietly made a habit of waiting for you to wake up. It's turned into one of my favourite parts of the day.

  • It was never awkward

    You said you don't connect with people quickly; but this just happened, on its own, from the very first day. I still can't quite believe how easy you are to talk to.

  • You teach Zumba

    An entire class follows your lead, and you shrug it off like it's nothing. I would sign up just to be terrible at it next to you.

  • You are ambitious about your career

    A physiotherapist who genuinely loves her work; and now doing research on physiotherapy for TB with an international university. That is real work that helps real people, and you talk about it like it's nothing. International exposure first, then your own clinic back in India; I already believe in both. And when that clinic opens, I am booking the very first appointment.

  • Nako evdha sorry mhanus

    You apologise for the smallest things; for being busy, for falling asleep, for being you. Tu jashi ahes tashi perfect ahes. Stop saying sorry.

  • You love to take care of your close ones

    You cook for your roommates like it's nothing. You look after your sister and her son, you take care of your parents; and somehow you still have room left to check on me. “Medicines ghetlis ka? Jevlas ka? Steam ghetlis ka?” You hold everyone together so quietly that nobody notices how much you carry. So let me be the one who notices. Kadhi tari mala tujhi kalji gheu de.

  • You feel everything, fully

    You worry about the strays out in the rain. You check on your people before yourself. You think that's too much; I think it's the best thing about you.

  • When I tease you

    You go quiet and send the exact right number of 😒. I have genuinely started teasing you a little just to watch it happen.

✿

You told me once that you want everything; more, nothing less.
Good. So do I. And the version of me you get is the same slightly-too-much one you already like.

Anyway; call me cute one more time. 🌸

For You About Me A Question

for AK

I've been
paying attention.

The first time we properly talked, it didn't feel like a first time at all. You said “dittoo,” and somehow that was that. Since then I've been quietly collecting little things about you — here are some of them.

  • You call me cute

    And I like it more than I'll ever admit out loud. I don't know why it lands the way it does — but it does, every single time. So please don't stop.

  • You love flowers

    So now I can't walk past them. I catch myself photographing the ones in the supermarket aisle — phone out before I've even decided to — just because I know they'll make you smile.

  • You can sleep anywhere

    Mid-sentence, mid-plan, mid-anything. And I've quietly made a habit of waiting for you to wake up. It's turned into one of my favourite parts of the day.

  • Coffee first, always

    Warm lemon-honey water before it, if you're being kind to yourself. And tea only when your dad makes it — yes, I remembered that too.

  • You dance, and you're good

    The kind of good you're far too modest to admit. I'm already bracing to embarrass myself trying to keep up with you.

  • You like life sorted

    Calm days, no chaos — and yet you'll still say “chal” to something completely unplanned. (Everything except treks. Noted, and respected.)

  • You quietly want more

    More than you let on. And you have this way of simply making things work when no one else can. It's a little unfair how impressive that is.

  • You feel everything, fully

    You worry about the strays out in the rain. You check on your people before yourself. You think that's too much — I think it's the best thing about you.

  • When I tease you

    You go quiet and send the exact right number of 😒. I've genuinely started teasing you a little just to watch it happen.

✿

You told me once that you want everything — more, nothing less.
Good. So do I. And the version of me you get is the same slightly-too-much one you already like.

Anyway — call me cute one more time. 🌸

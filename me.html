And a little
about me.

You already know most of this. But since I'm asking for a bit of your time, it feels only fair to say it plainly.

I'm the person who sends you a sunrise in the morning and a sunset at night, because I genuinely think you should see the good parts of my day. I run, I cook (mostly well), I get far too loud about my sport, and I get quiet when something actually matters.

I overthink, sometimes out loud, usually to you — and I'm working on it. But the same wiring that makes me overthink is the one that makes me pay attention: to what you like, what you don't, and the little things you'd never expect anyone to remember.

✿

What you can count on

  • ✿I'm loyal, and a terrible liar — you'll always get the real version of me.
  • ✿I keep my promises, even the small ones, even the inconvenient ones.
  • ✿I'll never make you shrink to fit — your goals stay yours, right beside mine.
  • ✿I'm patient. Good things are worth the wait, and so are you.
  • ✿And I will always, always ask if you've eaten. Always.

I'm not perfect. I'm just genuinely, fully in.

For You About Me A Question

about me

So, here's me.

You already know a lot of this by now. But since I am asking for a bit of your time, it's only fair I say it plainly.

I built this life mostly on my own; came far from home, figured it out the hard way, and kept it steady through all of it. Kahi flashy nahi tyat; just something I am quietly proud of. The rest is simple: I run, I cook (baryapaiki changla), I get far too loud about my cricket and football, a little dramatic sometimes, and I go very quiet when something actually matters.

Here's the real thing to know about me, though; I love in small, everyday ways. Good-mornings and good-nights, na-chukta. The second something goes right in my day ; a wicket, a good run, some silly compliment; you will be the first to hear it. I will bring you gossip nobody asked for. I will check if you reached home, if you have eaten, and I will ask twice if you leave me on read. Little things matter.

And yes, I will irritate you a little in between; the good kind, promise; just to keep us laughing. Long, pointless, 2 a.m. conversations that go absolutely nowhere? Tich tar khari maja ahe. That's my favourite place to be.

One fair warning: around my friends, I am the guy who roasts everyone, no mercy. Pan tu tension nako gheu; you are the exception, always. Tujhyasathi mi veglach asto: softer, cuter, fully yours.

✿

What you can count on

  • I am not perfect, and I won't pretend to be

    I will get things wrong sometimes; that's a promise too. But I don't dig in and defend my mistakes. I listen, I own them, and I actually change. Tu sang, mi badalto. I am always ready to be a better version of myself.

  • Loyal, and a terrible liar

    You will always get the real version of me; no games, no half-truths, no other versions running quietly on the side. Je asel te samor. What you see is genuinely, fully what you get.

  • Shabda dila mhanje dila

    I keep my promises; even the small ones, even the inconvenient ones. If I said I would do something, consider it done. You will never have to remind me twice, or wonder if I meant it.

  • Tujhi swapna tujhich rahtil

    Your goals stay yours; your ambition, your growth, your work. I am not here to slow you down or ask you to play smaller. I will just be the loudest one cheering, right there beside you.

  • You will always feel safe with me

    Majhya lokansathi mi khup protective ahe. With you, that means you never have to carry the heavy stuff alone. Whatever it is; I have got you, quietly, always.

We will figure out the rest later; right now, this is all I mean: I am all in. 🌸
